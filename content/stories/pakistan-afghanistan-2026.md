Pakistan launched Operation Ghazab-lil-Haq against Afghanistan in early March 2026, bombing Kabul and Kandahar in response to Taliban-linked cross-border attacks. The conflict rapidly escalated into a two-front squeeze as Afghanistan struck Pakistani military bases, including Nur Khan near Rawalpindi. A nuclear-armed nation is now in open war with its neighbour.
